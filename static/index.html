 LET’S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

It takes two to trade.

  1. Pick your team and browse potential trades.
  2. Swipe right or tap the heart if you’d take the deal. Swipe left or tap × to pass.
  3. If the other manager likes the exact same deal, you both get an in-app match alert.
  4. Talk it through in your group chat, then submit it on Sleeper.
How we find balanced trades

We compare recent non-zero weekly scores using your league’s scoring, blend small samples with a position baseline from Sleeper’s search rank, and consider starter fit, depth, and reported injuries. Trade scores are within 18% of one another; roster fit can still differ. These are estimates, not expert rankings, live projections, or a guarantee of fair value.

Byes, missing scores, injuries, and future performance create uncertainty. Review each deal for yourself. No draft picks are included.
